[size=5][b][color=#E8C88A]Easier Parry and Dodge While Blocking[/color][/b][/size]
<br />Makes parrying more forgiving or deadlier in [b]The Blood of Dawnwalker[/b].
<br />[list]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Parry timing window[/color][/b] configurable from [b]10% to 5,000%[/b], using the game's difficulty-adjusted baseline.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Dodge while blocking[/color][/b] defaults to On. Off restores vanilla dodge and guard behavior.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Perfect dodge window[/color][/b] is independently adjustable from [b]10% to 5,000%[/b].[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Riposte direction:[/color][/b] Choose Vanilla (Random), Opposite direction (default), or Same direction for the red opening after a directional parry. Independent of parry timing and dodge settings. The game still controls skill requirements, opening chance, duration and damage.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Dodge invulnerability window:[/color][/b] Independently scale normal and fatigued side/back dodge timers from [b]25% to 5,000%[/b]. [b]100%[/b] keeps normal duration. Protection ends when the timer expires or the dodge ends or is cancelled, whichever happens first.[/*]
<br />[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Change mod settings live.[/color][/b] Change settings live with [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/thebloodofdawnwalker/mods/271]Mod Setting Menu[/url][/*]

<br />[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Reporting Bugs and other issues[/color][/b][/size]
<br />
<br />If you encounter an issue, set [b]Logging[/b] to [b]Debug[/b] in this mod’s Mod Setting Menu settings, apply the change, reproduce the issue. Then start either a bug report in the Bugs tab on Nexus (preferred method for me to track things), or at least start a new thread in comments and send me [b]Dawnwalker/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/UE4SS.log[/b] from your game folder. Do not report unrelated bugs in other people's threads, please, this makes it impossible for me to track issues.
<br />
<br />
<br />[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Settings[/color][/b][/size]
<br />Enable HookProcessConsoleExec = 1 in your Vortex-managed UE4SS configuration in [b]Dawnwalker&#92;Binaries&#92;Win64&#92;ue4ss[/b][b]&#92;UE4SS-settings.ini[/b].
<br />Use [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/thebloodofdawnwalker/mods/271]Mod Setting Menu[/url] from the main menu. See SETTINGS.md (included with the mod) for all controls, first-use import and preference backups. Choose Off, Error, Warning, Info or Debug. Warning is the default; Off is silent. Debug records detailed troubleshooting information in Dawnwalker/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/UE4SS.log. Console settings commands are retired.

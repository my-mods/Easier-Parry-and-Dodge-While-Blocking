[size=5][b][color=#E8C88A]Easier Parry and Dodge While Blocking[/color][/b][/size]
Makes parrying more forgiving or deadlier in [b]The Blood of Dawnwalker[/b].
[list]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Parry timing window[/color][/b] configurable from [b]10% to 5,000%[/b], using the game's difficulty-adjusted baseline.[/*]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Dodge while blocking[/color][/b] defaults to On. Off restores vanilla dodge and guard behavior.[/*]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Perfect dodge window[/color][/b] is independently adjustable from [b]10% to 5,000%[/b].[/*]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Riposte direction:[/color][/b] Choose Vanilla (Random), Opposite direction (default), or Same direction for the red opening after a directional parry. Independent of parry timing and dodge settings. The game still controls skill requirements, opening chance, duration and damage.[/*]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Dodge invulnerability window:[/color][/b] Independently scale normal and fatigued side/back dodge timers from [b]25% to 5,000%[/b]. [b]100%[/b] keeps normal duration. Protection ends when the timer expires or the dodge ends or is cancelled, whichever happens first.[/*]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Change mod settings live.[/color][/b] Change settings live with [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/thebloodofdawnwalker/mods/271]Mod Setting Menu[/url][/*]
[/list]
[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Installation[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Vortex:[/color][/b] Install Easier-Parry-and-Dodge-While-Blocking.zip through Vortex, enable it and deploy.[/*]
[*][b][color=#A8D5C2]Manual:[/color][/b] Copy the archive's Dawnwalker folder into ...\steamapps\common\The Blood of Dawnwalker\, preserving the folder structure.[/*]
[/list]
[size=4][b][color=#E8C88A]Settings[/color][/b][/size]
Enable HookProcessConsoleExec = 1 in your Vortex-managed UE4SS configuration in [b]Dawnwalker\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss[/b][b]\UE4SS-settings.ini[/b].
Use [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/thebloodofdawnwalker/mods/271]Mod Setting Menu[/url] from the main menu. See SETTINGS.md (included with the mod) for all controls, first-use import and preference backups. Console settings commands are retired.
